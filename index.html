Day 1

Pass the campaign into Adapty

After a one-time SDK setup, the YouTube, Google or Apple Ads campaign each install came from arrives as a user attribute and picks the flow at first open. No app release.

Week 1

One flow per promise

Straight to Voicemail, privacy and the second texting number each get their own first screens; people who search your name keep today’s start.

Week 2

Judge each ad on what it earns

Each flow against today’s, measured on first purchases and what each install from that ad buys in credits, so spend follows the promise that pays.

All three phones run the whole flow with the five ideas. On Apple Ads you hold 84% of the US searches for your own name, “spoofcard”: the rest goes to other apps, worth defending. Your terms also include “funny call” and “prank call app - spoof dial”, which draw people looking for the pranks your recent one-star reviews ask for, not the privacy the app now sells. This layer carries no range: only a test per ad can give one.
